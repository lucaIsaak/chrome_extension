Welcome to Reef

Before Reef starts watching, here is exactly what that means

Reef has two jobs. It helps you focus by blocking distracting sites and growing a coral. And it shows you what websites collect about you. The second job needs to look at your browsing, so it stays switched off until you say yes.

What Reef records

On this device only.

  • The names of the websites you visit, like spiegel.de, and how often
  • Which companies those sites contact in the background, and what kind of data fields are sent to them
  • Cookie names and how long they last, never their contents
  • Whether scripts try to read details of your device (fingerprinting)
  • Page titles are read once to guess a topic, then thrown away
  • Shop sections such as "men's" or "women's", as counts only

What Reef never does

Not now, not later.

  • Send anything to a server. There are no accounts, no analytics, no ads
  • Store page content, passwords, form entries or the text of your emails
  • Record private (incognito) windows, even if you allow Reef there
  • Record adult, dating, medical or political-party sites at all
  • Guess political views, sexual orientation, health, religion or ethnicity

You stay in control

Older data is deleted automatically. You can also pause recording, export your data, or delete a single site or everything, any time, on the "your shadow" page. The focus timer, blocking and to-do list work without any of this.
