# Reef privacy policy

**Short version:** Reef keeps everything on your device. Nothing is sent to the developer or to anyone else.

## What Reef stores (locally, in the browser's extension storage)

**Focus tools:** your blocked sites, focus session history, to-do list and chosen background image.

**Shadow** (only after you press "Start recording" on the welcome page):

- Website names you visit (for example `spiegel.de`), visit counts and times of day
- Third-party companies each site contacts, with request counts
- Cookie names, domains and lifetimes (never cookie values)
- Names of data fields sent to third parties, with a shortened sample value
- Which browser features scripts used to read device details (fingerprinting)
- Topic categories guessed from site names and page titles. The titles themselves are discarded
- Counts of "men's" or "women's" shop sections visited, without the addresses
- Per-day counts of which tracking companies appeared on which sites, to build your weekly report
- The trackers you chose to block, how many requests that stopped, and how often focus mode turned a visit away
- Your "right / wrong" answers about the twin's guesses (they fade after 30 days)
- Your network choices: whether you joined the global or friends network, the sharing options and the privacy habits you ticked

## What Reef never does

- Send data to any server. There are no accounts, analytics, advertising or tracking of its own
- Store page content, passwords, form entries, cookie values or the text of emails
- Record private (incognito) windows, even if you allow the extension there
- Record adult, dating, medical or political-party websites
- Infer political views, sexual orientation, health, religion or ethnicity
- Sell or share data. The shareable twin image is made on your device and only leaves it if you download or copy it

## Permissions

| Permission | Why |
|---|---|
| `storage` | Keeps your settings and data on your device |
| `alarms` | Ends focus sessions on time and clears old data daily |
| `notifications` | Tells you when a focus session has finished |
| `declarativeNetRequest` | Blocks your chosen sites during a focus session, and the tracking companies you choose to block |
| `webRequest` | Lets Shadow see which third parties a page contacts. It only observes; it never changes or blocks requests |
| Access to all websites | Needed to redirect blocked sites, observe requests on any site and run the in-page detector that notices fingerprinting. On Gmail, a separate script only notices that a message was sent, to ask whether a to-do is done. It never reads emails |

## Your control

- Recording is off until you agree on the welcome page, and you can pause it any time
- Choose how long data is kept: 30 days, 90 days, 1 year or forever. Older data is deleted automatically
- Export your data, delete a single website, or delete everything, on the "your shadow" page
- Removing the extension deletes all of its stored data

## The avatar network

The network is currently a **demo with simulated people**. Nothing is sent anywhere. If a real network is ever added, it will be opt-in, will share only an anonymous name, a privacy score and the settings you choose to share (never sites, browsing or your twin), and this policy will be updated before it goes live.

## Accuracy

The "digital twin" shows crude guesses made from simple rules, to illustrate what websites and advertisers could infer. They are often wrong and are not facts about you.
